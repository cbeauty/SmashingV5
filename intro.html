This game is the official online, single player version of Beaveayn's S.M.A.S.H.I.N.G game of life

- A playful approach to developing healthy habits

 

 

 

Click the green play button in the top left corner to start, or one of the pink arrows to scroll through options, settings and instructions (if needed).

During the game a pause button in place of the play lets you return to this screen.
